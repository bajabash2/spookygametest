STRANDED

You are stuck in an endless furniture showroom. Build during the day. When the lights die, get high up - something is walking the aisles.

VR: hold GRIP to make your hands solid and swing your arms to move/climb. Y/X = build menu, trigger = select/place, A = rotate, B = cancel.
Desktop: WASD + mouse, Space jump, E menu, R rotate, Q cancel, click = select/place.
